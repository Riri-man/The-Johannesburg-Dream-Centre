Donate Money

Monetary funds allow us to keep the lights on at the office, as well as allow us to fund all other projects.
With donated funds we can pay our bills; purchase office supplies needed for our Adult Empowerment Project;
add a little extra to our monthly Grocery and Children’s outreaches; fund our Youth Empowerment projects, etc.
Each cent is spent appropriately to ensure that our community is served in the best way possible.
You can also choose which project to benefit from your donated funds. Simply add it in the payment reference.
For example: to benefit the Grocery Outreach, use “groceries” as your reference, etc.

You can donate funds via depositing it into our bank account, or by using your credit/debit card.
