    Our story

    About the Dream Centre

    Established in 2014, The Johannesburg Dream Centre is a community hub serving more than 500 families annually. We bring immediate support and lasting opportunity together under one roof.

    Our mission

    To alleviate poverty through immediate support and empowerment, giving people the tools and opportunities to build a stronger future.

    Our vision

    A Johannesburg with drastically reduced poverty, where individuals and families can look to the future with hope.
